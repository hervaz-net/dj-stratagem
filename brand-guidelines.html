Brand guidelines

Stratagem
Exchange

The B2B supply network for construction, by D&J Stratagem. Manufacturers sell to distributors, distributors sell to contractors, and contractors post what the job needs.

The mark

Three linked nodes, one supply chain.

A manufacturer and a distributor joined to a contractor, every node connected to the others.

The two white nodes are the upstream sellers. The amber node is where material lands: the jobsite. The rounded tile is brand green in every use. The mark is drawn on a 32-unit grid: tile radius 9, node radius 3, links 1.8 units at 55% white.

Clear space and size

Half the mark's width on every side. No type, rule, or image enters it.

Minimum size
8 mm print
20 px screen

Below 20 px the links close up. Use the tile alone, never the wordmark, at favicon sizes.

Backgrounds

Light canvas, white surface, or the dark band. Never on brand green.

Standing rule
Show what the product does. Never claim what it hasn't done.
No invented customers, logos, testimonials, or metrics. Sample listings, prices, and company names carry a visible sample label and are obviously fictional.
