Fleet cards in the Stratagem Exchange brand.

A design study for fuel cards issued to drivers and crews and coded to the job. It shows how the mark, palette, and type carry onto a card face, a controls panel, and a statement.

Concept only. Stratagem Exchange does not offer a fuel card program. Nothing on this page can be applied for, and every name and number is a placeholder.

Card specification

Format

CR80, 85.6 × 54 mm, rounded 3 mm corners. Matte laminate so the mark reads under forecourt lights.

Mark placement

Top left at 11 mm, with clear space of half its width. The tile flips to the dark band on the green card.

Type

Numbers in monospace, labels in the system sans at 600. No embossing, so the face stays flat and legible.

Controls panel · layout study

How a card's rules would be laid out in the app.

Product lock

Diesel cards for diesel and DEF, unleaded cards for unleaded.

Limits

Caps per transaction, day, and week, in dollars or gallons.

Hours and radius

Working hours only, within a radius of the yard or the active job.

Driver PIN and odometer

Gallons attach to a unit, not just a card.

Job coding

Every fill codes to a job number so fuel lands on the right project.

Exceptions

Weekend fills and out-of-radius swipes flagged for review.
