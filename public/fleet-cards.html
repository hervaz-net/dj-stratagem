Controls

Every card carries its own policy. Set it once when the card is issued and the pump enforces it.

Product lock

Diesel cards buy diesel and DEF. Unleaded cards buy unleaded. Anything else declines at the terminal.

Limits

Caps per transaction, per day and per week, in dollars or gallons. Raise one for a haul, and it reverts on schedule.

Hours and radius

Restrict fuelling to working hours and to a radius around the yard or the active job site.

Driver PIN and odometer

Each driver has a PIN and enters the odometer at the pump, so gallons attach to a unit, not just a card.

Job coding

Every gallon codes to a job number and a GL account, so fuel lands on the right project before the month closes.

Exception alerts

Weekend fills, tank overfills and out-of-radius swipes flag to the fleet manager within a minute of clearing.
