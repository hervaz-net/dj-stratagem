The operating system for construction growth — bidding, marketing, CRM and AI. This sheet governs every reproduction of the mark.

The mark

Monogram, hard hat, and three graded bands at forty-five degrees.

The bands are reinforcing bar seen in section — the material a bid is measured in. The hat sets the trade. The monogram carries the name. Read together they say what the product does before a word of copy is spent.

Construction

Band axis45° exactly — three parallels, equal pitch
BowlTrue circle: one radius, one centre
CounterConcentric, 0.61 of the bowl radius
CrownEllipse, 1 : 1.072 minor to major
TerminalsSwallowtail concave bite, two horns
TailCut at 45°, parallel to the band axis
Every overlap holds a constant void. Nothing in the mark touches anything else.

Clearspace

One band pitch, measured off the outermost tie, on all four sides. No type, rule or image enters it.

Minimum size
18 mm print
32 px screen

The mark holds at a thumbnail; the ties close last. Below 32 px the ties fill in — use the monogram-only cut instead.

Reversal

Two values only. No greys, no gradients, no outline.
