D&J Stratagem is a Los Angeles-based construction platform bringing the job lifecycle into one connected account.

LOS ANGELES · SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA
Why we’re here

The project is connected. The paperwork rarely is.

A contractor can find a project in one place, prepare a bid in another, chase a supplier by email, and still need to coordinate the crew and vehicles somewhere else. Each handoff costs time and context.

Stratagem brings those steps into one place. Not to replace the people who know the job, but to give them a clearer way to move it forward.

How we build

Tools with their boots on the ground.

Useful, accountable, and designed for the pace and pressure of real work.

01

Respect the work

Construction is a team sport. Our tools should make the work easier to coordinate, not add another layer of busywork.

02

Keep it connected

A job does not stop at the bid. We build around the full path from opportunity to delivery to the next customer.

03

People stay in control

Accounts are reviewed by a person. AI can prepare work, but humans approve before it runs.

Rooted here
